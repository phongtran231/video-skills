## Description
Tạo dáng, xoay khoe đồ, cận chi tiết chất liệu, kết tự tin. Kiểu TikTok affiliate haul.

## Structure
### hook: Tạo dáng ấn tượng
Open on a confident full-body pose that shows the whole outfit at once.

### setup: Reveal
A smooth spin or walk that shows the silhouette from every side.

### climax: Cận chi tiết
Close-ups of fabric, fit, buttons, shoes and accessories.

### payoff: Kết tự tin
End on a confident smile or a final pose toward the camera or mirror.

## Hook
A confident full-body pose.

## Pacing
Rhythmic, cut on the movement; each shot shows one part of the outfit.

## Dialogue
Optional: one friendly line about the outfit, TikTok style.

## Camera
Full-body vertical framing, a mirror shot with a phone, slow push-ins, close-ups on fabric texture, gentle arcs around the model.

## Lighting
Bright, soft daylight from a window, flattering and even, crisp fabric texture.

## Sound
Fabric swish, footsteps, light room tone; a trendy upbeat track fits.

## Avoid
The outfit stays the same in every shot; tasteful framing only. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode is a different outfit or occasion.
