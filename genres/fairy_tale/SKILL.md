## Description
"Ngày xửa ngày xưa…": thử thách, phép màu, bài học. Hợp hoạt hình 3D hoặc phong cách tranh.

## Structure
### hook: Hình ảnh kỳ diệu
Open on a magical image: a glowing tree, a talking animal, a mysterious old man.

### setup: Nhân vật và mong ước
A good-hearted lead, a simple life and a wish or a wrong to put right.

### turn: Thử thách
A test of kindness, courage or honesty.

### climax: Phép màu
Magic or help arrives because of the lead's goodness.

### payoff: Bài học
End on the reward for goodness and the lesson, in one image.

## Hook
A magical image.

## Pacing
Storybook rhythm: clear steps, a pause before the magic.

## Dialogue
Simple, warm storyteller speech in the film's language; short lines.

## Camera
Wide establishing shots of the world, gentle crane moves, close-ups for the magic moment.

## Lighting
Saturated storybook light, glowing magic, golden sunlight, moonlit blue nights.

## Sound
Wind, birds, a temple bell, rustling leaves; physical sounds for magic (a soft gust, a light crackle), never tonal words.

## Avoid
Original creatures and places, never known films or characters. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End as a new quest begins.
