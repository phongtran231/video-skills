# Genre engine: warm 1970s-80s family (年代, niên đại ấm áp)
The engine is FROM POVERTY TO PROSPERITY WITH FAMILY WARMTH: hard times in a village or small town, an arranged or "wrong" marriage that turns sweet, a gruff husband who dotes in deeds, not words, and a family that rises by work and wit.
- Period detail sells it: ration coupons, a bicycle, a thermos, a shared courtyard, a radio, a small stall that becomes a shop.
- The pressure comes from greedy relatives, gossiping neighbours and hard luck; the wins are a good harvest, a business idea, a child's school place, a wrong put right.
- Warmth over cruelty: the antagonists are petty, not monstrous; the payoffs are dignity and a better life. Gentle humour throughout.
