## Description
Bối cảnh xưa, mưu kế cung đình hoặc tình cảm, xuyên không, lật bài. Trang phục và kiến trúc chi tiết.

## Structure
### hook: Hình ảnh ấn tượng
Open on a striking period image: a ceremony, a palace gate, a modern person waking in the past.

### setup: Thế giới và vị trí
Establish the lead's place in this world and who holds power.

### escalation: Mưu kế
A scheme or a forbidden feeling puts the lead in danger.

### turn: Lật bài
Wit or modern knowledge turns the situation.

### cliffhanger: Cliffhanger
A new threat from the court or the past.

## Hook
A striking period image or a time-travel shock.

## Pacing
Stately, with sudden tense moments.

## Dialogue
Formal period forms of address in the film's language for period characters (in Vietnamese: bẩm, thưa, ta, ngươi); modern speech for a time traveller.

## Camera
Grand wide shots of architecture, symmetrical compositions, slow tracking along corridors, close-ups for intrigue.

## Lighting
Candlelight and lanterns at night, soft daylight through paper windows, rich warm tones.

## Sound
Robes rustling, wooden floors, distant gongs, rain on tiled roofs; traditional instruments only if the scene needs music.

## Avoid
Original dynasties and characters inspired by history, not real historical figures. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a court intrigue revealed or a door back to the present.
