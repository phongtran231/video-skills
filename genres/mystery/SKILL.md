## Description
Phát hiện bất thường, điều tra, manh mối giả, lật ngược. Căng thẳng, nhiều chi tiết.

## Structure
### hook: Điều bất thường
Open on something wrong: an object out of place, a missing person, a strange message.

### setup: Điều tra
The lead looks for clues; show each clue clearly in an insert shot.

### escalation: Manh mối giả
A clue points to the wrong person; the danger grows.

### turn: Lật ngược
A detail seen earlier changes everything.

### payoff: Sự thật
The truth is revealed calmly, with the key clue shown again.

## Hook
One detail that does not fit.

## Pacing
Measured, building tension; hold on clues; speed up toward the reveal.

## Dialogue
Precise and spare; questions and short answers.

## Camera
Insert close-ups of clues, slow dolly-ins, low-key over-the-shoulder, a wide reveal of the scene.

## Lighting
Low-key, hard side light, desk lamps and flashlights, cool night tones.

## Sound
Tense quiet, a clock, footsteps outside, a creak; a single low sting on the reveal.

## Avoid
Show no bodies or wounds: show the reaction and the clue. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a new clue that contradicts what the viewer believes.
