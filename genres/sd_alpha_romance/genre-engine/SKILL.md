# Genre engine: alpha and fated mate (werewolf romance, for the English market)
The engine is THE REJECTED ONE RISES: a heroine rejected by her fated mate or pack discovers a hidden power or bloodline, catches the eye of a stronger alpha or the lycan king, and makes those who rejected her regret it.
- Pack hierarchy is the power map: the alpha, the luna, the betas, the omega; rank challenges and the mate bond are the pressure and the payoff.
- Transformations and fights are suggested (glowing eyes, a shadow, a howl, the aftermath), never shown in full.
- Write in English with the genre's vocabulary (mate, luna, alpha, pack, mark), keeping every name, pack and place invented.
