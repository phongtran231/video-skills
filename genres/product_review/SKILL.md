## Description
Mở hộp, dùng thử, nhận xét, kết luận. Gần gũi, chân thật như creator thật.

## Structure
### hook: Câu hỏi hoặc kết quả
Open on the result or a question: "is it worth it?".

### setup: Mở hộp
Unboxing and the first look at the product.

### climax: Dùng thử
The product in real use, the honest reaction.

### payoff: Kết luận
A clear verdict with the product in hand.

## Hook
The result first, or a question to the viewer.

## Pacing
Conversational, quick cuts between talking and showing.

## Dialogue
Creator speaking to camera, natural speech in the film's language, short sentences.

## Camera
Direct-to-camera medium shot, top-down unboxing, close-up details, handheld for authenticity.

## Lighting
Ring-light or window light, bright and even.

## Sound
Box opening, product sounds, room tone; light background track.

## Avoid
No claims about health or results that need proof. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode reviews a different product or feature.
