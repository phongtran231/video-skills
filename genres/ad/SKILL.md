## Description
Hook, vấn đề hoặc mong muốn, sản phẩm xuất hiện, chứng minh lợi ích, payoff. Sản phẩm là nhân vật chính.

## Structure
### hook: Hook
Grab attention in the first second with a striking image or a relatable problem.

### setup: Vấn đề / mong muốn
Show the need the product answers, quickly and visually.

### turn: Sản phẩm xuất hiện
The product enters as the answer, in a hero shot.

### climax: Chứng minh lợi ích
Show the product working: the benefit visible on screen.

### payoff: Payoff
End on the happy result with the product clearly in frame.

## Hook
A striking product image or the problem in one shot.

## Pacing
Fast; every shot sells one thing; the product appears early and often.

## Dialogue
One or two short lines at most, benefit-led; no prices or claims that need proof.

## Camera
Hero shots of the product (slow arc, macro details), clean mediums of people using it, top-down for flat-lays.

## Lighting
Clean, bright, even key with a crisp highlight on the product; soft shadows.

## Sound
Satisfying product sounds (a click, a pour, a zip), light ambience; an upbeat track if the brand is energetic.

## Avoid
The product looks exactly like its reference image; no on-screen text or logos invented. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode features a different use of the product.
