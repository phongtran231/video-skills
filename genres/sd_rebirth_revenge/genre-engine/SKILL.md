# Genre engine: rebirth and revenge (重生逆袭, trọng sinh báo thù)
The engine is A SECOND CHANCE WITH FOREKNOWLEDGE: the lead dies or is betrayed, wakes years earlier with every memory, and rewrites fate step by step.
- Episode 1 shows the betrayal and the death (never graphically) and the waking: the date, the room, the realisation.
- Foreknowledge is the weapon: the lead knows who will betray, which deal fails, which document matters, and acts one move ahead; the audience enjoys watching the trap close on people who do not know.
- The goal is bigger than revenge: restore the family's honour, save the one they lost, build what was taken; each episode wins one piece.
- Keep the rules consistent: what the lead remembers, what has already changed because of them.
