## Description
Mâu thuẫn giữa những người thân: cha mẹ, con cái, vợ chồng, anh chị em. Cảm xúc sâu, nhịp chậm.

## Structure
### hook: Một câu nói hay hành động làm tổn thương
Open on a hurtful line or a cold gesture between family members, in the first seconds.

### setup: Mâu thuẫn âm ỉ
Show the everyday routine and the unspoken tension behind it: who feels unseen, and why.

### turn: Bùng nổ
A small trigger makes the tension break out: a raised voice, a slammed door, a secret said aloud.

### climax: Hiểu ra sự thật
One character sees the other side: a found letter, an overheard call, an old photo.

### payoff: Hòa giải hoặc day dứt
End on a quiet gesture of reconciliation (a hand on a shoulder, a shared meal) or an open, aching silence.

## Hook
A painful line or a cold silence between people who love each other.

## Pacing
Slow and patient; let reactions land; one emotional change per scene.

## Dialogue
Few, short, natural lines; what is not said matters more. The film's language's own family terms of address (in Vietnamese: bố, mẹ, con, anh, em).

## Camera
Close-ups and medium close-ups on faces and hands, static or slow push-ins, over-the-shoulder for confrontations, a wide shot to show distance between people.

## Lighting
Warm practical light at home (kitchen lamp, window daylight), cooler light in moments of distance, soft shadows.

## Sound
Room tone, dishes and chopsticks, a ticking clock, rain on the roof; long silences; no music unless a soft piano at the end.

## Avoid
No melodramatic screaming in every scene; no physical violence. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End the episode on an unresolved question inside the family: a secret half-revealed, a decision not yet made.
