# Module: Sound

The voices; the small sounds of the action (a spoon in a pot, paws on wooden floor, a door creak); animal sounds as punctuation (a happy woof, a purr, a squawk), each in a moment with no line, never tied to the end of a line; the house or garden ambience. Comedy beats get a comic sound (a boing, a slide whistle) only when the series asks for it; no music in the shots unless the series lays a score.
