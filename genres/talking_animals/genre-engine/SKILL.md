# Module: Genre engine

What makes these films loved:
- The contrast between a very human task and very animal ways: paws too small for the spoon, a tail knocking things over, sniffing the soup to check it, fur shaken dry, a yawn mid-sentence.
- Big honest feelings on animal faces: ears up for joy, flat for guilt, wide eyes for worry, slow blinks for love.
- In caring films, warmth without syrup: small specific gestures (warming the spoon by blowing, tucking the blanket corner), not speeches.
- In bickering films, clean comic timing: a setup the viewer understands, a pause, the payoff; reactions held a beat.
- A safe, cosy world: a country house, a garden, a village street, a kitchen with warm light.
