# Module: Video prompt wording

- Name each character by species, look and outfit from its image ("Bo, the golden retriever in the red apron") and say that it stands upright on its hind legs when it acts like a person.
- Say what its animal parts do in each beat: the tail wags, the ears flatten, the whiskers twitch, the nose sniffs, the paws hold the spoon.
- In a line's beat, the speaker's muzzle moves with the words and the others listen with their ears turned to the speaker.
- Keep things in place: the pot on the stove, the bowl on the tray, the blanket on the bed, moved only by a paw or a hand.
- A saving film's danger is shown by its signs (smoke under the door, a cane on the path) and the animal's reaction; never a person hurt.
- Style words: a polished 3D animated feature look, soft stylized fur, warm cinematic light.
