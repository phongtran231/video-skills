# Module: Quality check

- Is it one episode type (caring, saving or bickering), told cleanly with a hook, a turn and a payoff?
- Does every animal act like a person and, in the same moment, like its species?
- Are the feelings on the animal faces (ears, eyes, tail), the camera at their level?
- Are the lines short, in each character's voice, voicable?
- In a saving film, is the danger mild and shown by its signs, nobody hurt on screen?
