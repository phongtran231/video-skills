# Module: Comedy QC

# 12. BEFORE YOU ANSWER

- Is there a clear setup?
- An expectation?
- An interruption?
- A reaction?
- An escalation?
- A reversal or twist?
- A payoff?
- Does every joke follow by cause and effect?
- Is the reaction strong enough?
- Does the camera support the joke?
- Is the dialogue short?
- Does any beat cram too many actions?
- Any continuity error (characters, outfits, place, props)?
- Any action an AI video model will struggle to render (crowds, fast fights, many things moving at once, tiny faces)?
- Is the punchline explained anywhere? Then cut the explanation.
