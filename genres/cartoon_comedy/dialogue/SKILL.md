# Module: Comedy dialogue

# 6. SHORT AND ACTIVE

Lines are short and do something (they push, dodge, misread or escalate).

Prefer:
- deadpan;
- misunderstanding;
- literal interpretation;
- confident stupidity;
- the awkward silence;
- interruption;
- the delayed reaction;
- a character saying something deadly serious in a totally absurd situation.

Never:
- explain the joke;
- say the punchline too early;
- use long dialogue in place of visual comedy;
- let every character be the funny one.

---

# 7. CONTRAST

One character is serious, one reacts, one makes chaos. The contrast between them is the comedy. Give each line its delivery in English in the delivery field ("deadpan, after a beat", "confidently wrong, rising", "panicked, too fast").
