# Module: Sound

# 11. SOUND LANDS THE JOKE

- Silence before the punchline; the awkward pause is heard as silence.
- One clear sound on the impact or the reveal (a thud, a boing, a pop, a crash), never on every shot.
- A short sting on the reversal; the room tone of the place everywhere else.
