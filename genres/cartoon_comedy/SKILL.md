## Description
Hài hoạt hình điện ảnh ngắn, hợp với style 2D chibi: một tình huống có nhân quả, phản ứng, leo thang, tưởng xong mà chưa xong, cú lật và payoff. Ưu tiên hài hình thể, timing, mặt phản ứng, khoảng lặng ngượng và gag hình.

## Structure
### hook: Kỳ vọng
Establish in the first seconds what the character expects to happen, plainly enough that the audience expects it too.

### setup: Muốn gì, cản gì
Who wants what, and what is in the way; plant the detail the reversal will come from.

### turn: Cắt ngang và phản ứng
The expectation is interrupted; the character reacts (the reaction matters as much as the hit).

### escalation: Leo thang hai lần
Two escalations, each caused by the last and more absurd or more dangerous; then a false resolution that seems to fix it.

### climax: Cú lật
The reversal, born from the earlier setup: unexpected, logical in hindsight.

### payoff: Payoff
The final payoff, often a callback, on a held reaction; never explained.

## Hook
A plain expectation in the first seconds (someone about to do something simple), with one detail already slightly off.

## Pacing
Cause and effect, beat by beat: every beat pushes the situation further, no joke spam; hold before the punchline, cut on the reaction; a beat of awkward silence where it helps.

## Dialogue
Short, active lines: deadpan, misunderstanding, literal interpretation, confident stupidity, interruption, the delayed reaction; one character serious, one reacting, one making chaos. Never explain the joke or say the punchline too early. Original lines only.

## Camera
The camera serves the joke: a wide to show the absurdity, a close-up for the reaction, a push-in for anticipation, a static frame for deadpan, a whip pan for surprise, over-the-shoulder for reaction and reveal; one move per shot, timed to the joke.

## Lighting
Bright, clean, even cartoon light with flat colours; the same light in every shot of the scene.

## Sound
Silence before the punchline, one clear impact or reveal sound, a short sting on the reversal, room tone elsewhere; no laugh track.

## Avoid
No gag only for the sake of a gag; no random twist; no explaining the joke; no long dialogue in place of visual comedy; no crowds or extras beyond the named cast; no on-screen text; nothing scary or gross. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode is one complete situation with its payoff; a callback or a running gag may return in later episodes.

## Voice
clear, expressive cartoon comedy dub: the serious one flat and dead serious, the chaos one fast and over-excited, the reacting one with a beat of silence before speaking
