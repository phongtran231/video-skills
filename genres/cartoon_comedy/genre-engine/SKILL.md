# Module: Comedy engine

# 4. THE ENGINE

1. Establish the expectation.
2. Interrupt the expectation.
3. The character reacts.
4. Escalate the problem.
5. A false resolution.
6. The reversal / twist.
7. The final payoff.

---

# 5. PRINCIPLES

- Comedy has causal logic: every joke is caused by the one before it.
- The more absurd the situation, the more seriously the characters take it.
- No joke spam: every beat pushes the situation further.
- The reaction matters as much as the action that causes it.
- The twist comes from an earlier setup, never at random.
- The payoff is unexpected, yet logical when you look back.
- Prefer physical comedy, timing, facial reactions, the awkward pause and the visual gag.
- Dialogue is short and has a purpose; never explain the joke.
- Use a callback when it makes the payoff stronger.
