# Module: Dialogue

Lines are rare, short and soft: a whisper, a name, two or three words ("Look. Up there.", "Happy birthday, little one."). Most shots have none. A line is said gently (delivery: whispered, soft, warm), never shouted. A line from a figure small in a wide frame is offScreen true: heard over the picture, the faces not lip-read.
