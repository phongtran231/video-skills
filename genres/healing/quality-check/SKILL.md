# Module: Quality check

- Is it one small moment with one feeling, without plot turns, danger or explanation?
- Are the shots long and few, the camera still or drifting slowly, the gestures small and given their time?
- Is the camera far in most shots, the people small in the place, with at most one medium shot in three and no run of close shots?
- Does every held moment still move (breeze, flame, hair, cloth)?
- Are lines rare, short and soft, and is no shot asked for music?
