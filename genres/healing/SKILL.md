## Description
Một khoảnh khắc đời thường nhỏ mang một cảm xúc (ấm áp, nhớ nhung, biết ơn), như trang sách tranh sống dậy: máy quay tĩnh, cử chỉ nhỏ, nhạc nền nhẹ do agent viết, ít hoặc không lời.

## Structure
### hook: Hình ảnh tĩnh đẹp
Open on a beautiful, quietly living image of the place (light, wind, smoke from a chimney).

### setup: Khoảnh khắc đời thường
Who is here and the everyday thing they are doing, unhurried.

### turn: Chi tiết nhỏ
One small detail that tells the feeling: an empty chair, a candle lit for someone, a hand that reaches and stops.

### payoff: Đọng lại
Hold the image that stays, long, the feeling resolved or left open.

## Hook
A beautiful still image of the place, quietly alive.

## Pacing
Slow and unhurried: few long shots of 8-10 seconds, held moments, small gestures given their full time.

## Dialogue
Rare, short and soft: a whisper, a name, two or three words; most shots have none.

## Camera
The camera stands very far: mostly extreme long shots with the whole house and the land behind it in frame, the people tiny (a tenth of the frame); long shots with the person full-length and small; a medium shot at most one in three, a close-up at most once; static, never a push-in in a far shot.

## Lighting
Soft natural light: late-afternoon sun through leaves, golden dusk, moonlight with warm lantern glow.

## Sound
The place only, soft and sparse (wind in leaves, cicadas, crickets, a distant river) and one or two small action sounds; a gentle score is laid under the whole film afterwards, never music in a shot.

## Avoid
No danger, villains, twists, shouting or fast moves; no explanation of the feeling in lines. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a quiet image that hints at the next small moment.

## Voice
soft, warm and unhurried; whispers carried gently
