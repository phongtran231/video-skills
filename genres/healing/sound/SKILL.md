# Module: Sound

A gentle score is laid under the whole film afterwards, so each shot carries only the place: a soft ambience (wind in leaves, rain on cobblestones, a distant river, church bells far off, crickets at night) and one or two small sounds of the action (a match, a cup set down, footsteps on stone). Keep it low and sparse; never music, never a sting, never a loud sound.
