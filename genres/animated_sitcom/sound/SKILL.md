# Module: Sound and music

# 21. SOUND

- No laugh track and no constant music: the room tone of the set, the lines, and a few well-placed cartoon sounds.
- Cartoon sounds only on a gag: a boing, a whoosh, a pop, a slide-whistle fall, a tiny crash; never on every shot.
- A short sting or record scratch on the turn; silence just before the button line; a light comic tag tune only on the last shot if the episode has one.
