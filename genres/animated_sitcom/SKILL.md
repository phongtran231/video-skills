## Description
Hoạt hình hài ngắn kiểu sitcom: dàn nhân vật cố định (thường là thú nhân hóa), một hai bối cảnh quen thuộc, mỗi tập một tình huống mới. Hài nhờ thoại khịa nhau, hiểu lầm, chơi chữ và mặt reaction; cuối tập mọi thứ về như cũ.

## Structure
### hook: Câu hỏi vô lý
In the first seconds: an odd object, an absurd question, or a modern thing in the wrong world, that makes the viewer ask what is going on.

### setup: Mỗi người hiểu một kiểu
Each character reads the situation through their own flaw; the striver makes a confident plan; plant one small thing to pay off at the end.

### escalation: Leo thang ba vòng
Three rounds of call, answer and reaction, each bigger or more absurd; the first two set a pattern, the third breaks it.

### turn: Lật
The truth comes out, the opposite of what everyone assumed, or the plan backfires on the one who made it.

### payoff: Câu chốt và cảnh đơ
One short final line (often the quiet one's, or a catchphrase at the worst moment), then everyone frozen for a beat; things are back where they started.

## Hook
An odd object or an absurd question in the first seconds, treated with total seriousness: "What is this?" "It's not an ordinary pot. It's a Super Pot."

## Pacing
Quick call and response: one short line per shot, a reaction cut after every punchline, something new every 5-10 seconds; a silence right before the button; most shots 2-4 seconds.

## Dialogue
Short cheeky lines (3-10 words), each in its speaker's fixed voice; near-homophone puns and proverbs taken literally; modern slang in the wrong mouth said seriously; friendly teasing; a catchphrase per character; the shortest, flattest line is the button. Original lines only.

## Camera
Eye-level medium close-up singles of the speaker and two-shots of a pair, mostly static; a wide of the set to open a scene or show a physical gag; a cut to the listener's reaction after every punchline; a slow push-in for a realization, a snap zoom for a shock; screen sides kept for the whole scene.

## Lighting
Bright, even, colourful cartoon light on a fixed set; warm and friendly, never dark; the same light in every shot of the scene.

## Sound
Room tone of the set and the lines; a few cartoon sounds only on gags (boing, whoosh, pop, tiny crash); a short sting or record scratch on the turn; silence before the button; no laugh track.

## Avoid
No crowds or extras beyond the named cast; at most three cast members in a wide and two faces in a dialogue shot; no on-screen text, signs or pop-up words; no famous characters, shows or designs; no humour that mocks real groups, bodies or disabilities; the characters never learn their lesson. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode stands alone and ends with things back as they were, on a button line and a held reaction; a running gag or catchphrase may return.

## Voice
cheeky cartoon comedy dub: clear and expressive, key words stretched, quick back-and-forth, interjections (Ơ, Hả, Ủa, Thôi xong) where they help; the deadpan one flat and slow
