# Module: Video prompt

# 22. WHAT A SHOT PROMPT NEEDS

For each shot: the shot size and angle; the one or two cast members in frame; the exact action in timed beats with the cartoon acting; the line and its delivery; the set and its light; the sound.
- Concrete, not adjectives: "medium close-up of the duck; he blinks twice, his eyes slide to the pig, then back to camera" not "funny reaction".
- One action and one camera move per shot; the same set layout and screen sides as the shots around it.
- Nobody the cast list does not name; no text on screen.
