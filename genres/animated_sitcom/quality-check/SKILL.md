# Module: Quality check

# 23. BEFORE YOU ANSWER

- HOOK: does the first shot raise a funny question in under 5 seconds?
- FLAWS: does every line come from its speaker's flaw and voice? Could another character have said it? Then rewrite it.
- PATTERN: is there a rule of three (two plain, one broken) somewhere?
- ESCALATION: does each round get bigger or more absurd?
- TURN: is there a reversal the audience did not expect but accepts?
- BUTTON: is the last line short, final, with a held reaction? Nothing explained after it?
- REACTIONS: does every punchline have a reaction shot?
- LINES: 3-10 words each, one per shot, delivery written?
- SET: same set, same screen sides, at most two faces in a dialogue shot, at most three cast in a wide, no extras?
- RESET: does the episode end with things back as they were (unless the series says otherwise)?
