## Description
Một ngày, một chuyến đi, một trải nghiệm. Góc nhìn cá nhân, nhịp nhẹ nhàng.

## Structure
### hook: Khoảnh khắc đẹp nhất
Open on the best moment of the day or the place.

### setup: Bắt đầu
The start of the day or the trip.

### escalation: Trải nghiệm
A few varied moments: food, places, people.

### payoff: Kết ngày
A calm, satisfied ending: sunset, home, a final thought.

## Hook
The most beautiful or surprising moment.

## Pacing
Relaxed, montage-like; each shot a new small moment.

## Dialogue
Personal, casual lines to camera; a few words each.

## Camera
Handheld POV, selfie-angle talking shots, wide establishing shots of places, close-ups of food and details.

## Lighting
Natural light: morning, golden hour, city lights at night.

## Sound
Real places: street, café, waves, market; light chill track.

## Avoid
Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Each episode is a new day or place.

- thư giãn
