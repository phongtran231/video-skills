## Description
Tuổi học trò, thi cử, tình bạn, crush, trưởng thành. Tươi sáng, gần gũi với khán giả trẻ.

## Structure
### hook: Khoảnh khắc quen thuộc
Open on a moment every student knows: a test returned, a crush walking by, a bell ringing.

### setup: Đời thường
Show the friends, the classroom, what the lead wants or fears.

### turn: Biến cố nhỏ
Something changes: a failed exam, a quarrel with a best friend, a confession note.

### climax: Vượt qua
The lead chooses to face it: studies at night, apologises, speaks up.

### payoff: Trưởng thành
End on a small, bright victory or a lesson learned with friends.

## Hook
A relatable school moment.

## Pacing
Lively, with a quiet moment before the turn.

## Dialogue
Natural teen speech in the film's language, light slang, short lines between friends.

## Camera
Tracking shots following students in corridors, medium shots of groups, close-ups for the inner moment.

## Lighting
Fresh daylight through classroom windows, sunny schoolyard, warm sunset for the ending.

## Sound
School bell, chatter, pencils on paper, cicadas in summer.

## Avoid
All characters on screen are clearly at least 18 when a scene is romantic; keep it innocent. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a school event coming up or a friendship in doubt.
