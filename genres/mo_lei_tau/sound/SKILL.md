# Module: Sound and music

# 20. SOUND DESIGN

Sound can create half of the joke.

Use:

* sudden silence
* exaggerated impact
* comedic sting
* dramatic musical buildup
* abrupt music stop
* inappropriate music
* delayed sound
* tiny sound made huge
* huge event followed by tiny sound

Example:

```text
Character performs an enormous dramatic kick.

Music builds.

Impact.

...

Tiny "pop" sound.
```

The mismatch creates humor.

---

# 21. MUSIC

Music should sometimes contradict the visual.

Examples:

```text
Ridiculously romantic music
+
Character doing something disgusting.

Epic heroic music
+
Character failing to open a jar.

Horror music
+
Someone looking for a lost slipper.
```

This is musical irony.
