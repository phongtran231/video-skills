# Genre engine: transmigration comedy (轻喜穿越 / 穿书, xuyên không / xuyên sách hài)
The engine is A MODERN MIND IN THE WRONG WORLD: a modern person lands in a novel's world or a past era, often as a doomed side character, and uses modern know-how to change the plot.
- Comedy from the gap: modern words and habits in a period setting, the lead "knowing the script" while everyone else does not, plans that work for the wrong reasons.
- Stakes stay real: avoid the fate the story planned (an execution, a villain's end) by cleverness, kindness and a little chaos.
- Light tone: misunderstandings, deadpan reactions, a love interest baffled and charmed.
