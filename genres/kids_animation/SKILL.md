## Description
Nhân vật dễ thương, gặp vấn đề, thử và sai, học được bài học. Màu tươi, rõ ràng.

## Structure
### hook: Điều buồn cười
Open on the cute lead doing something funny.

### setup: Vấn đề
A simple problem a child understands.

### escalation: Thử và sai
Two or three funny attempts that fail.

### climax: Cách giải đúng
The right way: sharing, asking for help, being patient.

### payoff: Bài học
End happy, with the lesson shown, not lectured.

## Hook
A funny moment with a cute character.

## Pacing
Clear and simple; one idea per scene.

## Dialogue
Very simple speech in the film's language for children; short, kind lines.

## Camera
Eye level with the characters, mediums and wides, smooth simple moves.

## Lighting
Bright, colourful, soft shadows.

## Sound
Playful sound effects, cheerful ambience.

## Avoid
Nothing scary or dangerous for children to copy. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a small new adventure tomorrow.
