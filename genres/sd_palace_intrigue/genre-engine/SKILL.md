# Genre engine: palace and mansion intrigue (古装宅斗 / 宫斗, cổ trang trạch đấu / cung đấu)
The engine is SCHEME AGAINST SCHEME: in an inner court or a great family's mansion, a wronged daughter (or concubine) outwits rivals who use poison, rumour, frame-ups and favour.
- Every episode one scheme is set against her and turned back on its maker, with evidence, a witness or a trap of her own.
- Rank, etiquette and face matter: who may sit, who must kneel, who speaks first; humiliation and triumph play out in those rules.
- Poison and punishments are implied, never shown: a cup set down untouched, a servant taken away, a verdict read.
