# Genre engine: divorce and regret (离婚后悔 / 追妻火葬场, ly hôn hối hận)
The engine is THE DISCARDED ONE THRIVES: a husband throws her away for someone else; she leaves with her head high, and is revealed as an heiress, a genius, or simply someone who builds a life without him; he regrets and chases her, and has to earn even a hearing.
- Episode 1: the divorce (papers, a cruel line, the other woman's smile) and her first step out of the door.
- Her rise is shown in concrete wins (a design chosen, a company bought, a family reunited); his regret in small humiliations (his calls refused, seeing her admired).
- She decides the ending; forgiveness, if any, is hard-won and on her terms.
