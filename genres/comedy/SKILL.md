## Description
Tình huống đời thường, hiểu nhầm leo thang, twist và punchline. Ngắn, nhanh, dễ viral.

## Structure
### hook: Điều vô lý ngay đầu
Open on an absurd line or action that makes the viewer ask what is going on.

### setup: Tình huống bình thường
Establish the normal situation and what the character wants.

### escalation: Hiểu nhầm leo thang
A misunderstanding grows with each beat; every attempt to fix it makes it worse.

### turn: Twist
Reveal what was really going on.

### payoff: Punchline
End on the funniest reaction or a final line that flips the joke.

## Hook
An absurd line or a weird action, no context.

## Pacing
Fast; cut on the laugh; reaction shots carry the jokes; the punchline is the last shot.

## Dialogue
Snappy, everyday speech in the film's language, timing matters; let a reaction answer a line.

## Camera
Bright wides and mediums for physical comedy, quick cut-ins to faces for reactions, a static camera so the timing reads.

## Lighting
Bright, even, cheerful; no dark moods.

## Sound
Everyday sounds exaggerated slightly (a squeak, a thud), comic pauses; no laugh track.

## Avoid
No humour that mocks appearance, disability or a group of people. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a new absurd situation set up for the next episode.
