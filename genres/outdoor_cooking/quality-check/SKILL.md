# Module: Quality check

- Is the whole dish there, its steps in the real order, each with its real technique, and no later step relying on one that never happened?
- Does every ingredient, the fire and every tool keep its state and place from shot to shot, changing only by a hand on screen?
- Are most shots on the hands, food and fire, the cook's face rare and never close?
- Is each step given the time to be seen, never rushed?
- Is the sound the work and the place, with no speech or music?
