# Module: Video prompt wording

- The camera line gives the height, the angle, the size, the move and the lens: "Close-up at board level, low three-quarter angle, static, 85mm, shallow depth of field" or "Macro, top-down, slow push-in, 100mm" or "Medium at ground level from beside the fire pit, static, 50mm".
- Name every thing the shot shows by its state and its place: "the raw beef slices, red and glossy with marinade, in the wooden bowl at the left of the board", "the flat grey stone over the fire, hot, a thin film of oil shimmering on it".
- Each beat is one hand action with its tool and its result: "the right hand lays a slice of marinated beef on the hot stone; it sizzles and white smoke rises" (0-3s), "the tongs turn the slice over, its underside now brown with a crust" (3-6s).
- The rules line holds the states and the places for the whole shot: what is raw and what is cooked, what rests where, that nothing moves unless a hand moves it, that the fire keeps burning, that only the hands and forearms (and the food) are in a close frame.
- The opening describes the state the step starts from, the ending the state it leaves (the next shot opens on it).
- Light: say where it comes from ("the low sun behind the stone, the smoke glowing", "warm ember light from below, the forest soft and green behind").
- The audio line: the close sounds of the work and the place (knife on wood, sizzle, crackling fire, water poured, the stream, birds); never music or speech in a cooking close.
