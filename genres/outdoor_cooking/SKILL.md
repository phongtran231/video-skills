## Description
Một, hai hoặc ba người cắm trại và nấu trọn một món giữa thiên nhiên (đa số trong rừng, sát bên bờ suối; đôi khi bờ hồ, đồng cỏ, rừng tuyết): dựng trại, nhóm lửa, sơ chế, ướp, nấu, nêm nếm, bày ra và ăn cùng nhau, đủ từng bước theo thứ tự thật; xen các khoảnh khắc của nhân vật và cảnh đẹp quanh trại; cận tay và đồ ăn, tiếng xèo xèo, lửa tí tách, rất ít lời, không nhạc.

## Structure
### hook: Món ăn / ngọn lửa
Open on a striking close image of the food or the fire in the place, or the place waking as the cook arrives.

### setup: Chuẩn bị
Set up the camp: lay out the tools and ingredients, wash the pot, stone or pan, build and light the fire, prepare and cut the ingredients, marinate.

### escalation: Nấu
Cook step by step in the real order: heat, oil, lay on, sear, turn, add, season, baste; each step seen done.

### payoff: Bày và nếm
Finish, plate and taste the dish in the place; end wide on the camp and the land.

## Hook
A striking close image of the food or the fire in the wild.

## Pacing
Calm and unhurried: one step per shot of 5-10 seconds, each action seen from start to end; the order and states of the recipe hold.

## Dialogue
A cook alone never speaks; two or three people share a rare short natural line now and then.

## Camera
Cooking steps in close and medium shots on the hands, the board, the pot, the stone and the fire (low angle, soft background); between them medium and wide shots of the people at the camp and wide shots of the landscape around it; faces never in a close-up.

## Lighting
The real light of the place (soft morning through pines, low golden sun, grey snow light, blue dusk) and the warm light of the fire on the food.

## Sound
Close, crisp sounds of the work (knife on wood, sizzle on hot stone, crackling fire, water poured, salt falling) over the soft sound of the place (a stream, wind in the pines, birds); no speech, no music in the shots.

## Avoid
No talking, no rushing, no magic: every ingredient, the fire and every tool changes state only by a hand on screen, in the real order of the recipe. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on the finished dish in the place, ready for the next meal.
