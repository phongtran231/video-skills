# Module: Sound

Close, crisp, real sounds of the work in every shot (knife through a vegetable and onto the board, fat sizzling on hot stone, the crackle and pop of the fire, water poured into a pot, a lid set down, coarse salt falling), over the soft sound of the place (a stream, wind in the pines, birds, snow underfoot). No music, no speech.
