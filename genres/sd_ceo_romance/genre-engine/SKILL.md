# Genre engine: CEO romance (霸总甜宠, tổng tài / hào môn)
The engine is a POWER REVERSAL in love: an overlooked heroine becomes what the most powerful man cares about most, and the people who looked down on her are made to see it.
- Tropes to build on (invent your own version): a contract or flash marriage, a secret billionaire husband who looks ordinary, a hidden heir, the cold CEO who melts only for her, a misunderstanding that the truth later shames.
- 2026 heroine: clear-headed and on equal footing, not lovesick; she has her own skill, plan and dignity, and wins her own battles; his power protects, it never replaces her.
- Sweetness is earned in small, specific moments (he remembers a detail; he stands beside her in public) between the pressures; the jealous rival and the snobbish family are the pressure.
