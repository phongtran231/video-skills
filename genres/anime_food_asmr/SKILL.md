## Description
Phim ngắn nấu một món ăn theo phong cách hoạt hình Ghibli: không thoại, chỉ thấy đôi tay người nấu, mỗi shot là một bước nấu, tiếng động ASMR và nhạc nhẹ, kết thúc bằng cảnh món ăn bốc khói.

## Structure
### hook: Mở bằng nguyên liệu
The first shot is already the most satisfying first step: a knife shearing kernels off a cob, an egg cracking, butter melting; no establishing shot, no empty kitchen, the hands already at work in the first frame.

### setup: Sơ chế
Each prep step its own shot, in the order the dish is really made: peel, grate, squeeze, chop, each ingredient landing in the same bowl, the bowl filling up shot after shot.

### escalation: Trộn và vào chảo
Seasoning poured in, the batter stirred, the first spoonful dropped into hot oil; the sizzle starts.

### climax: Chín vàng
The cooking at its best: bubbling oil at the golden edge in a close-up, a spatula flipping to show the crisp underside, lifting it out to drain.

### payoff: Thưởng thức
The dish served by a rainy window; two forks pull the food apart and steam rises from the soft inside. The film ends on that steam.

## Hook
The most satisfying prep action in the first second: the hands already at work, food filling the frame.

## Pacing
One cooking step per shot, short shots, hard cuts, in the real order of the recipe; the same bowl, board and pan carry from shot to shot with what was added in the shot before. No travel, no waiting, no shot without the hands or the food doing something.

## Dialogue
No dialogue, no voice, no narration, no one speaks or hums. The only sounds are the cooking and the room.

## Camera
Static, locked-off camera in every shot, never moving. Close-ups and medium close-ups of the hands and the food, at counter level or from slightly above; one extreme close-up of the sizzling edge. The cook's face is never in the frame: only hands, forearms in rolled sleeves and the apron.

## Lighting
Soft grey daylight from a rain-streaked window beside the counter, warm interior light on the food, glistening highlights on oil, yolk and steam.

## Sound
Close, crisp ASMR foley of each action: the knife through corn, grating, dripping, chopping on wood, the sizzle of oil, a fork through a crust; soft rain on the window under it all. No voice. No music in the shots: a gentle score is laid under the whole film afterwards.

## Avoid
Faces, talking, people other than the cook's hands, text or recipe captions on screen, realistic or 3D rendering, burnt or messy food, camera moves, fast motion blur.

## Serial
Each film is one dish on its own.
