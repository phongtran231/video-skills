# Genre engine: hidden identity and face-slap (隐藏身份打脸, thân phận ẩn / vả mặt)
The engine is HUMILIATION, THEN REVEAL, THEN PAYBACK, escalating rung by rung: an underestimated lead (a son-in-law, a maid, an intern, a delivery driver) is secretly powerful.
- Each cycle: someone humiliates the lead in public; the lead endures with a hint of calm; a sign of the real identity appears (a call, a bow from a stranger, a card); the humiliator is shamed in front of the same people.
- Each humiliator outranks the last; the identity is revealed a layer at a time, never all at once until a marker episode.
- The lead stays restrained and decent: the payback is exposure and consequence, not cruelty.
