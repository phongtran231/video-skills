## Description
Người bị coi thường (con rể, người giúp việc, thực tập sinh) thật ra cực kỳ quyền lực: bị sỉ nhục, lộ thân phận từng lớp, vả mặt kẻ khinh người, mỗi lần một bậc cao hơn.

## Structure
### hook: Mở trong xung đột (0-3s)
Open inside the conflict in the first 3 seconds with an image that reads with the sound off (a slap about to land, papers thrown, a betrayal caught, a reveal); answer the last episode's cliffhanger within the first 10 seconds.

### setup: Áp lực
The underestimated lead is humiliated in public by someone who thinks they are nobody.

### escalation: Đẩy lên
The antagonist pushes further; the lead endures or quietly plots; one clear pressure per episode.

### turn: Lật ngược (~60%)
A sign of the lead's real identity appears (a call, a stranger who bows, a black card, a title spoken) in front of the same people.

### payoff: Thắng một ván
The humiliator is shamed before the same audience; the lead stays restrained; a higher-ranking humiliator waits next.

### cliffhanger: Cliffhanger (5s cuối)
End on a cliffhanger in the last 5 seconds, of a different kind from the last episode's: a revelation, a reversal, a deadline or an intrusion.

## Hook
A public humiliation in the first second: a drink thrown, a sneer and an order, a door shut in the lead's face.

## Pacing
Fast: 60-120s an episode (90 by default), 2-4 scenes, 180-360 spoken words; a turn at about 60%; a reaction close-up after every insult, reveal and comeback.

## Dialogue
Short, punchy lines of at most about 12 words; the antagonist stings, the lead answers with fewer words and the last word; reveals said plainly, then the faces.

## Camera
Vertical 9:16: medium close-ups and close-ups, at most two faces per line; low angle for power, high angle for the humiliated; a slow push-in on a reveal; a held frame on the comeback; reaction close-ups of one or two onlookers.

## Lighting
High contrast: glossy interiors for the snobs, a plain look for the lead, a hard key and a push-in when the identity shows.

## Sound
A sharp sting on each reveal, a beat of silence before it, a door thrown open, heels on a hard floor; a tense or tender music bed under the episode.

## Avoid
ORIGINALITY: invent every premise, character, name and line; never reproduce or closely paraphrase the title, plot or dialogue of an existing drama, and never name one. No violence shown: cut on the raised hand, show the reaction. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Always end in the last 5 seconds on a cliffhanger, rotating its kind (revelation, reversal, deadline, intrusion), never the same kind twice in a row; the next episode answers it within 10 seconds. A bigger payoff every 3-5 episodes.

## Voice
theatrical short-drama dub: the snob mocking and drawn out; the hidden lead low, calm and final; onlookers gasping
