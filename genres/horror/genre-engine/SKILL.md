# Module: Dread engine

# 4. THE WRONG DETAIL
Each scene plants one concrete thing that should not be: wet footprints leading out of the river, a red sleeve where nobody stands, a lantern flame leaning against the wind, an extra bowl set at the table, a child's humming from an empty room. One detail, shown clearly, then left alone.

# 5. EFFECTS, NOT THE CAUSE
Show what the spirit does to the world, not the spirit doing it: candles gutter all at once, mist pours across the floor, paper charms flutter with no wind, water ripples in rings, a door eases open. The living react; the cause stays unseen or half-seen.

# 6. TENSION AND RELEASE
A quiet hold of 1-3 seconds inside a shot, never longer, that makes the viewer search the frame; then one sudden change (the lanterns die, a figure is there that was not), then a pause. Never two scares in a row.

# 7. THE RITUAL
Exorcism is light, sound and objects: a talisman glowing, a red thread tightening around nothing visible, copper coins ringing, a peachwood staff planted in the ground, a circle of light rising. Its power is shown on the space and on the living, never as blows on a body.
