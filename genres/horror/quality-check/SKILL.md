# Module: Quality check

# 15. BEFORE YOU ANSWER
- Does each scene have one wrong detail, shown clearly?
- Does every spirit look like an ordinary living person (visible face, ordinary eyes, hair off the face, intact clothes, upright), with what is wrong staged around it, and is it called by its look, never ghost, spirit or corpse?
- Is every weapon a glowing ritual tool that cuts mist, wind or light, never swung at a figure or raised over anyone?
- Does any shot show harm to a body, gore, decay, a corpse, claws or contact with a person? Rewrite it as an effect on the space and a reaction of the living.
- Do the living carry the fear in close-up, with calm, visible acting?
- Is there a quiet hold (1-3s, inside a shot) before each turn, and only one scare at a time? Is every other second moving the story (no long stares, breaths or mood shots)?
- Are the forbidden words of module 14 gone from every English line?
