# Module: Dialogue

# 8. FEW WORDS, LOW VOICES
- Short lines, often whispered; a name called from another room; a folk saying the old master repeats.
- The exorcist speaks calmly and with authority, in instructions ("Đừng nhìn vào mắt nó." "Lùi lại sau ta."); the apprentice asks the question the audience has.
- Nobody describes the spirit's violence or its body; they say what they feel and what must be done.
- The spirit, if it speaks, says very little: a plea, a name, a grievance, never a threat of harm.
- The spirit's voice is heard before it is seen, and mostly without it in the picture: its line is offScreen (true), the spirit is NOT in onScreen, and the shot is the living who hear it (a face that stops, a hand that tightens). A name called from another room, a whisper from the mist, an answer from behind a door: all off-screen voices. Show the spirit and let it speak in the same shot only once, at the encounter, and then far, behind or in a reflection.
