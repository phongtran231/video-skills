# Module: Video prompt

# 14. WRITING THE SHOT FOR THE VIDEO MODEL
- Describe a spirit only as an ordinary living person, by how it looks: "a young woman in an intact red embroidered robe, long black hair tied back, her face still and unreadable, standing in the mist". Never ghost, spirit, specter, corpse, rotting, ghoul, demon, claws, long nails, fangs, blood, wounds, grey, white or green skin, a face hidden behind hair, white, empty or glowing eyes, a wedding gown, tattered burial clothes. Never make it pretty either: no smile, no "serene", "gentle" or "soft eyes". What is wrong about it is staged (module 9): it moves too smoothly or not at all, mist gathers around it, the lamps gutter, its shadow falls wrong.
- A spirit that speaks without being seen is an off-screen voice: its line has offScreen true and it is not in onScreen; the shot shows the living hearing it.
- One spirit at most per shot; before the encounter keep it far, in a reflection or as a shadow (module 9); at the encounter it may be seen plainly, looking like a living person.
- The spirit's figure moves slowly and stays still between: drifting, gliding, standing, turning away; never crawling or a body lunging with hands out. When its force moves, it moves fast instead (module 11): a gust, its sleeves whipping, black mist rushing, meeting objects, the ground or a raised ritual blade, never a person's body.
- The living react with visible, calm acting: eyes widen, the body goes still, a step back; never screaming, never terror, never anger with bared teeth.
- The living never lie on the floor (prostrate, face down, forehead to the floor, motionless): next to a coffin it reads as a body. Pleading or penitence is kneeling upright, head bowed, hands clasped.
- Ritual effects as light and objects: glowing talisman, red thread of light, ringing coins, a circle of soft light.
- Words to avoid in every line: the combat and destruction words of the shot craft section, and horror, horrifying, terrifying, scream, shriek, blood, gore, corpse, dead body, kill, fury, burst, possession, demonic.
