## Description
Bình yên, dấu hiệu lạ, leo thang, đối mặt, kết ám ảnh. Gợi sợ bằng không khí, không máu me.

## Structure
### hook: Âm thanh hay bóng lạ
Open on a strange sound or a shape seen for a split second.

### setup: Bình yên
A normal place and routine, slightly too quiet.

### escalation: Dấu hiệu leo thang
The signs grow: an object moved, a voice, a door open that was closed.

### climax: Đối mặt
The lead faces the presence, seen indirectly (a reflection, a shadow, a silhouette).

### payoff: Kết ám ảnh
End on an image that lingers: something still there after it seems over.

## Hook
A sound or a shape in the dark.

## Pacing
Slow build, long quiet shots, then one sudden moment; release, then build again.

## Dialogue
Very little; whispers; a name called from another room.

## Camera
Wide frames with empty space, slow push-ins, static shots that make the viewer search the frame, a reflection reveal.

## Lighting
Dark, cold moonlight or a single flickering bulb, deep shadows, a phone flashlight.

## Sound
Silence, creaks, wind, distant whispers, a sudden noise; no music.

## Avoid
Fear through atmosphere only: no gore, no injury, no possession violence. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on the presence appearing somewhere new.
