# Module: Storytelling

# 1. A RULE, A WRONG, A GRIEVANCE
Every episode has: a folk rule (a taboo, a ritual, a place one must not go), a wrong that breaks it (water flowing upstream, a wedding never finished, a grave moved), and a spirit with a grievance the audience can pity as well as fear. The mystery is WHY she returns; the episode answers a little and asks more.

# 2. THE SHAPE OF DREAD
Calm (a normal place, slightly too quiet) -> the wrong detail (noticed by one person, dismissed by another) -> the signs grow (a sound, a moved object, a light that bends) -> the encounter, seen indirectly -> a lingering image that is still wrong after it seems over. Release the tension once (a laugh, a false alarm) before the real turn.

# 3. THE LIVING CARRY IT
Fear is told through the living: the exorcist who knows and stays calm, the apprentice who is afraid and tries anyway, the villager who hides the truth. Their choices drive the plot; the spirit's presence presses on them.
