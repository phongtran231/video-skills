# Module: Sound

# 13. THE SOUND OF THE UNSEEN
- Silence is the main instrument: before the turn, drop the ambience. Start that shot's audio with "near silence:" and name the one or two small sounds left ("near silence: a floorboard creak, one drip of water"); the render then takes the scene's bed out of that shot. Use it once or twice an episode, right before the turn, never for a run of shots.
- Wind through reeds, water dripping, a distant temple bell, paper charms rustling, a lantern creaking on its hook, copper coins ringing.
- Under the encounter, a physical low sound the place can make: wind moaning through a gap in the boards, a deep creak of old timber, water lapping slowly; never a hum, a drone, a note or a tone (video models turn those into music), never screams, never gore sounds.
- After the scare, the ordinary sound comes back slowly (crickets, the river): the next shot's audio names the first ordinary sound to return.
- Hard words stay out of audio too: a hard gust, a sharp gust, a sudden gust; never a violent or savage one.
