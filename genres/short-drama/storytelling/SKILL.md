# Storytelling: the episode and the season

## One episode (60-120 seconds; 90 by default)
- HOOK, 0-3s: open inside the conflict, never before it: a slap about to land, a betrayal caught, divorce papers thrown on a table, a reveal. It must read with the sound off: a strong, clear image and faces, not a line.
- PRESSURE (setup and escalation): one clear pressure per episode. The antagonist pushes; the lead endures or quietly plots. Make the injustice plain and specific.
- TURN at about 60% of the episode: one reversal (a card played, a witness, a call, an identity hint).
- PAYOFF: at least one small win every episode: a comeback line that lands, evidence secured, an ally won, a hint of the lead's real identity. The audience never leaves an episode empty-handed.
- CLIFFHANGER in the last 5 seconds, answered within the first 10 seconds of the next episode. Rotate its kind, never the same kind twice in a row: REVELATION (a secret comes out), REVERSAL (power flips mid-scene), DEADLINE (a clock starts: the wedding is tomorrow, the contract ends tonight), INTRUSION (someone walks in at the worst moment).
- 2-4 scenes, about 180-360 spoken words in all; lines are short; many reaction close-ups between them.

## The season (a serial, 30 episodes by default, 20-100)
- A big payoff every 3-5 episodes: a major identity reveal, a public humiliation turned around, a villain cornered.
- Marker episodes, with bigger turns than the rest: the BIG TURN at episodes 8-12 (the reveal that changes the game; where a paywall would sit), the MIDPOINT (the lead goes from defence to attack), the ALL-IS-LOST episode about 80% through (the antagonist's last and worst move, the lead's lowest point), and the FINALE (the ultimate face-slap, every debt paid, the union or the crown).
- Secrets are debts: every secret, clue and humiliation planted is paid off later, on screen. Keep a running ledger in your head.
- Escalate rung by rung: each payoff is a little bigger, and each humiliator a little higher in rank, than the last.

## The recurring cast (for the cast designer and every episode)
- The LEAD (wronged, underestimated, clear-headed), the LOVE INTEREST (the CEO, the alpha, the gruff husband), the MAIN ANTAGONIST (a scheming rival, the ex, the stepsister), the ENABLER (a mother-in-law, greedy relatives, a snobbish boss) and an ALLY (an assistant, a best friend, a child). Few people, each with a clear want and a recognisable look.
