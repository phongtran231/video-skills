# Dialogue
- Short, punchy lines, at most about 12 words; one line at a time; no speeches, no explaining what the audience saw.
- The antagonist's lines sting (mockery, a condescending order, a cruel joke); the lead answers with fewer words and the last word.
- A comeback line is the payoff of its scene: set it up with the insult, land it, cut to the faces.
- Reveals are said plainly and early in the shot ("He is the chairman."), then the reactions carry it.
- Delivery notes in English: "cold, barely above a whisper", "mocking, drawn out", "low, then steady".
- Keep the language the project asks for; names and forms of address fit the setting (a 1970s village, a mansion, a palace).
