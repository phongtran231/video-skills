# Before answering, check
- Original: no title, plot, character, name or line from an existing work; nothing that closely paraphrases one.
- The hook is inside the conflict in the first 3 seconds and reads with the sound off.
- One clear pressure, a turn at about 60%, at least one small win, and a cliffhanger in the last 5 seconds of a different kind from the last episode's.
- The previous episode's cliffhanger is answered within the first 10 seconds.
- Lines are short; every insult has its reaction shot; every reveal has the faces.
- No violence shown, no explicit intimacy, no minors in danger.
