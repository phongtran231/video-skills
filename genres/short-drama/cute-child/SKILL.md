## The cute-child tone (萌宝), when it is the project's tone
A clever, adorable child (about 5-7) is a recurring ally: they see through the adults, say the truth nobody dares to, match-make their parents, and unmask the antagonist with a child's logic. They are never in real danger and never the target of cruelty on screen; their scenes are warm and funny, a relief between the pressure.
