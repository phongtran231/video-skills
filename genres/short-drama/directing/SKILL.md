# Directing for a vertical phone screen
- Faces are the story: medium close-ups and close-ups, at most two faces holding a line; onlookers are one or two named characters or a soft background, never a crowd of faces.
- After every insult, reveal and comeback: a reaction close-up (the antagonist's smile freezing, the onlooker's eyes widening, the lead's still eyes).
- Power in the frame: low angle for whoever holds power, high angle for the humiliated; a slow push-in on a reveal; a held static frame on the comeback.
- The hook frame is a strong, readable image in the first second: the raised hand, the thrown papers, the tearful face, the ring dropped.
- Violence is never shown: cut on the raised hand, show the reaction and the sound; no blood, no blows landing.
- Glamorous interiors for the rich (marble, glass, chandeliers), warm cramped rooms for the poor, period sets for era and palace stories; the setting tells who holds power.
- Subtitles are burned in by the app: keep the lower third of the frame clear of faces when you can.
