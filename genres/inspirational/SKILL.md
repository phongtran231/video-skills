## Description
Khó khăn, nỗ lực, khoảnh khắc thành công, thông điệp. Chân thật, gần gũi.

## Structure
### hook: Khó khăn thật
Open on a real struggle: early morning work, a rejection, tired hands.

### setup: Mục tiêu
Show what the lead is fighting for.

### escalation: Nỗ lực lặp lại
A short series of efforts and setbacks, each a different image.

### climax: Khoảnh khắc thành công
The effort pays off in one clear moment.

### payoff: Thông điệp
End on a quiet, proud image that carries the message.

## Hook
A real, relatable hardship.

## Pacing
Montage-like in the middle, slower at the success.

## Dialogue
Few lines; maybe one line of voice of conscience or a short exchange.

## Camera
Light handheld, close-ups of effort (hands, sweat, eyes), a rising crane or wide at the success.

## Lighting
Natural light: dawn blue, harsh noon, warm sunset at the success.

## Sound
Real sounds of work and the city; a hopeful track can rise at the end.

## Avoid
Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on the next goal or a new obstacle.
