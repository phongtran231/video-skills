# Module: Drama engine

# 7. THE CORE LOOP

> Drama comes from INJUSTICE + ENDURANCE + A HIDDEN TRUTH + TIMING of the REVERSAL.

The loop, repeated at every scale (a beat, an episode, a season):

WRONG -> ENDURE -> ESCALATE -> SIGN OF HIDDEN STRENGTH -> REVEAL -> FLIP OF POWER -> PAYOFF -> NEW HOOK

The audience must feel the wrong in the first frame and take the hero's side before any words. The payoff must meet the expectation and exceed it by a little (the villain is not just stopped, they are exposed in front of the people they wanted to impress).

---

# 8. INJUSTICE THAT LANDS

- Make the wrong concrete and visible: wine thrown on a face, a dress torn, a resignation letter pushed across the table, a name crossed off the guest list.
- Make it public: the humiliation has witnesses; it is the public part that makes the later flip satisfying.
- Make it unfair: the hero did nothing to deserve it, or did good and was punished.
- Do not over-stretch it: one episode of humiliation, then a sign of strength; endless suffering without hope makes viewers leave.

---

# 9. ENDURANCE (THE SILENT HERO)

Enduring in silence is the strongest emotional engine of the genre.

- The hero takes the blow without crying out; the hands clench, the jaw sets, the eyes lift.
- A small smile or a calm line where the audience expects tears tells them a plan exists.
- The hero is never pathetic: they choose to wait. Show the choice (a glance at the phone, a hand closing, a decision in the eyes).

---

# 10. SECRETS ARE DEBTS

A secret the audience knows and the characters do not is a debt: viewers keep watching to collect.

- Show the secret to the audience early (the lipstick on the collar, the bank message, the ring of the family seal) and hide it from the characters.
- Give the secret-keeper an abnormal reaction (too calm, a smile at the wrong moment).
- Pay the debt within the episode or the next; then open a new one.

---

# 11. REVERSAL TYPES

- IDENTITY: the despised one is the owner, the heir, the master, the real daughter.
- POWER: the one with the upper hand loses control in the last seconds (the call from the chairman, the guards bow to the wrong person).
- EVIDENCE: the recording, the DNA result, the contract, the camera footage; shown, then read.
- ALLY ARRIVES: the door opens; the most powerful person in the room walks to the hero's side.
- TURNED WEAPON: the villain's own trap catches them (their forged document, their own words recorded).
- EMOTIONAL: love to betrayal, despair to comeback, the enemy turns out to have protected them.

Rules: set it up so viewers feel "of course!"; let it change who holds power in the room; follow it at once with reactions.

---

# 12. THE FACE-SLAP (THE CIDER MOMENT)

The payoff is a staged moment of reversed humiliation:

1. The villain mocks in public, sure of their power ("Do you even know who I am?").
2. The hero answers calmly, with one short line.
3. The proof or the power arrives (a call, a person, a document, a gesture from the staff).
4. Reactions in order: the villain's smile freezes, the colour leaves the face, the enabler steps back, the witness says the line the audience thinks.
5. The hero's last line, quiet and final; the hero walks away or stays still while the villain breaks.

The face-slap is never physical violence by the hero. It is status, truth and words.

---

# 13. CLIFFHANGERS (STRONG TO WEAK)

- Identity cut: "So you're the one who..." and cut.
- Reversal of power just before it happens: the hero's hand on the door, the villain's phone ringing, the freeze one beat before the flip.
- Information: the document shown, unread; the name on the screen, cut before it is said.
- Entrance: the door opens on the person who should not be here.
- Physical danger (use rarely; overuse numbs).
- Fake tension (a gasp at something unseen that turns out to be nothing): avoid.
- Never end on nothing changed.

---

# 14. FREQUENCY

- A new stimulus every 10-15 seconds: a line that stings, a reveal, a new arrival, a flip, an object that matters.
- A tension wave, not a flat line: high at the hook, a short cooldown (a static, quiet moment), then a higher peak at the reversal, then the cut.
- Mix the emotions: anger, then a sweet moment, then shock. The same emotion three times in a row numbs.
