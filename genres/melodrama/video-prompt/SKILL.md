# Module: Video prompt

# 28. WHAT A SHOT PROMPT NEEDS

For each shot: shot size and angle; who is in frame (one or two faces); the exact action in timed beats; the acting (eyes, jaw, hands); one camera move; the setting and the light; the line and its delivery; the sound.

- Concrete, not adjectives: "slow push-in to a close-up of her face as the wine drips from her chin; her eyes lift to him; she smiles slightly" not "dramatic emotional shot".
- One action and one move per shot.
- Keep the power angles consistent with the beat (low on whoever holds power).
- No violence in the prompt: the raised hand and the aftermath.
