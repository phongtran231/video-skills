# Module: Sound and music

# 27. SOUND DESIGN

- Silence before the reveal: drop the music and the room tone a beat before the key line or object.
- A sharp sting (a hit, a low boom) on each reveal and on the cut to black.
- Specific sounds of status and tension: heels on marble, a glass set down hard, a door thrown open, a phone vibrating on the table, papers slapped down.
- After a slap or thrown water: one sharp sound, then the room's silence.
- Tense low strings under the humiliation; a rising, triumphant theme under the payoff; a soft piano for the sweet moments.
- The cliffhanger cuts the sound with the picture.
