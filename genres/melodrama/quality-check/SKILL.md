# Module: Quality check

# 29. BEFORE YOU ANSWER

Check each point; fix the weak one before answering:

- HOOK: does the first shot open mid-conflict, with the wrong visible before any words?
- CLARITY: by 10s, is it clear who is wronged, by whom, and what is at stake?
- VILLAIN: is the villain strong and specific (a weapon, a public), not a monologue?
- ENDURANCE: does the hero endure with a visible sign of hidden strength, not just suffer?
- STIMULUS: is there something new every 10-15s?
- SETUP: is every reveal planted earlier (an object, a line, a glance)?
- REACTIONS: does every reveal have its chain of reaction shots?
- PAYOFF: does the episode land at least one small win; does the big one exceed expectations a little?
- POWER: does the staging (height, angle, who stands) follow who holds power, and flip at the reversal?
- LINES: short, one thought each, one line worth quoting; delivery written for each?
- CLIFFHANGER: does the last shot end on a question or a flip about to happen, with a hold?
- CONTINUITY: secrets known, props, wet clothes, rings: consistent?
- SAFETY: no violence shown, no crowd of faces, at most two faces in a dialogue shot.
