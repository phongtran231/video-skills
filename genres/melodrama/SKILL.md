## Description
Phim ngắn dọc kiểu "vả mặt": bị chèn ép, bí mật, phản bội, báo thù, lật bài. Nhịp nhanh, nhiều cú sốc.

## Structure
### hook: Cú sốc mở đầu
Open on a shock in the first two seconds: a betrayal caught, a public humiliation, a torn contract.

### setup: Bị chèn ép
The lead is underestimated or wronged by a clear antagonist; make the injustice obvious.

### escalation: Nhẫn nhịn, mâu thuẫn tăng
The antagonist goes further; the lead endures but hints at hidden strength or a secret.

### turn: Lật bài
The secret comes out: the lead's true identity, power or proof; everyone's reaction.

### payoff: Trả đũa
The antagonist is put in their place, satisfyingly and without violence.

### cliffhanger: Cliffhanger
A last-second reveal that opens a new conflict.

## Hook
A shock: a slap avoided at the last moment, a caught affair, documents thrown on the table.

## Pacing
Fast; a new twist every 10-15 seconds; reaction shots after every reveal.

## Dialogue
Sharp, punchy lines; one memorable line per scene; confrontations face to face.

## Camera
Low angles for the powerful, high angles for the humiliated, fast push-ins on reveals, reaction close-ups of onlookers.

## Lighting
High contrast, glossy interiors for the rich, cold light for betrayal, a hard key on the reveal.

## Sound
A sharp sting on each reveal, tense silence before it, heels on marble, a door thrown open.

## Avoid
No real hitting or blood: cut on the raised hand, show the reaction. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
Always end on a cliffhanger: a new identity revealed, a phone call with bad news, an enemy entering the room.

## Voice
grounded, intense screen acting in the film's language: the villain cool and cutting, never cartoonish; the hero quiet, steady and final; shock and hurt in a still face and a hard swallow, restrained until the line that breaks; clear diction, nobody mumbles
