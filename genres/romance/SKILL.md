## Description
Gặp gỡ, rung động, trở ngại, khoảnh khắc thật lòng. Hình ảnh đẹp, cảm xúc nhẹ nhàng.

## Structure
### hook: Gặp gỡ tình cờ
Open on a meet-cute: a collision, a shared umbrella, a wrong order, a look across a room.

### setup: Rung động
Small moments of attraction: a smile held too long, a touch of hands, a nervous laugh.

### turn: Trở ngại
Something keeps them apart: a misunderstanding, a rival, a departure, family.

### climax: Khoảnh khắc thật lòng
One of them takes the risk: a confession, running after the other, a gesture that says it all.

### payoff: Bên nhau hoặc chia xa
End together in a beautiful image, or apart with a bittersweet memory.

## Hook
A meet-cute or one charged look.

## Pacing
Gentle; linger on looks and small gestures; slow motion for the key moment.

## Dialogue
Few words, warm and a little shy; the confession is one short line.

## Camera
Over-the-shoulder pairs, close-ups of eyes and hands, slow tracking side by side, a wide shot of the two in a beautiful place.

## Lighting
Golden hour, backlight and rim light, soft bokeh city lights at night, warm and flattering.

## Sound
Soft ambience (café, rain, wind in trees), heartbeat-quiet moments; a gentle romantic track is fine.

## Avoid
Keep affection tender (holding hands, a hug, a forehead touch); no intimate scenes. Video models break on many simultaneous movements, crowds, fast fights and tiny faces; they refuse gore, explicit violence, intimacy and minors in danger. Suggest, do not show: shadows, sounds, reactions, the moment before and after.

## Serial
End on a romantic uncertainty: a message unanswered, a rival appears, a promise to meet.
