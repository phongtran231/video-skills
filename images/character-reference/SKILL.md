## Animal
FRAMING: exactly one animal (the one described), its whole body from nose to tail, in a three-quarter side view, centered and filling most of the frame. A real animal with the natural anatomy and posture of its species (a cat on four paws, a bird perched or standing on its feet): never standing upright like a person, no human hands, no clothes, shoes, accessories or props, no human expression. Its eyes open and clearly visible, calm alert pose, sharp and evenly lit, no motion blur. Plain seamless light-grey studio background, soft even lighting, no scenery, no other animals or people.
