## Block
STORY SPINE (the screenwriter's; the story the viewer must follow; moments by id): every moment marked mustShow is on screen in some shot whose action shows it as its "show" says, with its full force (an attack lands and is met, a binding pins and strains, a reveal is seen), in the order given; a moment's cause is shown before it; never soften a mustShow moment into a calmer one or reduce it to a line. Each shot's "moments" lists the ids of the moments it shows; every mustShow id is in some shot's moments.
