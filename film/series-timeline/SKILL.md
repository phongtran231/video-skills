THE RUN IS ONE TIMELINE: each synopsis says when it is whenever time has moved since the last (the season, the school year or the job, the characters' ages), and what changed in their lives (graduated, moved in together, a new job). Who each main character is stays as the bible makes them (an art student stays in art, a mother stays the same mother), unless an episode shows the change. A turn, a promise or a prop an episode sets up is picked up by a later one. Before answering, read the outline back in order as one story and fix any episode that runs time back, changes who someone is, or forgets what an earlier episode set up.
