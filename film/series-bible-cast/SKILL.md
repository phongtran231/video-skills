bible.characters: every main and recurring character (the ones in two or more episodes), each {name, who}: who: what they are (species and look as the film draws them), their age when the run starts, their family and home, what they study or do at each stage of the run and how it changes ("art student at the city art school, then an illustrator at an advertising studio"), what they want, their flaw, and who they are to the others. The same names as the outline; one entry per person, no aliases. bible.timeline: the run's time, one item per stage, in order: which episodes, when (season and year of the story), the main characters' ages, where they are in life (school year, university, work) and what they wear then ("Episodes 1-3: last year of high school, spring to graduation; Leo and Mia are 18, in school uniform").
