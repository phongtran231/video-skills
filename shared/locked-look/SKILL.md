LOCKED CHARACTER PROFILE: each character's appearance is one permanent English profile, in this order: age and gender; height in cm and build; skin tone; hair length, colour, style and texture (and facial hair or clean-shaven); outfit with exact colour, fit and fabric for top, bottom and footwear; accessories with material and colour. Do not list facial features (no face shape, jawline, cheekbones, eyes, eyebrows, nose or lips): the reference image sets the face. Their appearance is their main outfit, worn in every scene whose wardrobe does not put them in another of their outfits. Use concrete words (e.g. "deep purple", "knee-length"), never vague ones (e.g. "stylish", "nice"). Appearance never describes actions, mood, lighting or setting. LOCKED LOCATIONS: list every place in locations (at most the allowed number), each with a name and an English description of the place type, time of day, light direction and colour, colour palette, ground or floor texture, and 3-5 fixed features in fixed positions told by the place itself, never by the picture (e.g. "a wooden lifeguard tower on the left as you face the sea, a row of three palm trees behind the sand", "windows along the left wall as the class faces the chalkboard"; never "frame left" or "screen left", because the camera turns between shots). Every scene.location is exactly one of these names. Scenes in the same place keep the same time of day and light unless the story moves time on. Never introduce a character, prop or place in a shot that is not in characters, objects or locations.
