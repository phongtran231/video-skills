CHARACTER FORMS: when a character appears in another form (an animal, creature, older self, disguise), list that form as its own character entry named "Name (form)", e.g. "Dần (cóc)", with formOf set to the base character name; every other character has formOf "". Each entry's appearance and imagePrompt describe ONLY that one form, never the other (the base entry must not mention the animal and vice versa). In shots, onScreen names exactly the form that is visible. Appearance describes only body, face, hair and clothes, never lighting or setting. A change of clothes, costume or outfit is NOT another form: keep one entry, its appearance in its main clothes; list the other clothes the story puts them in under its outfits (each a short name and the clothes in English: "wedding day": "an ivory satin floor-length gown, a lace veil, pearl drop earrings, ivory heels"), and each scene's wardrobe names who wears which outfit in it ([] when everyone is in their main clothes). Only for a change the story shows or needs (a wedding, a uniform, night clothes, a disguise of clothes): a scene in a new place or on a new day keeps their clothes unless the story changes them. A character's outfits they already have (pinned, or listed with the recurring characters) are used by their exact name whenever the clothes are the same or close: a navy peacoat is the outfit they already have for it every time, never a new entry. A new outfit only for clothes clearly unlike every one they have and unlike their main look; named by the clothes ("navy peacoat", "hospital gown"), never by a moment or an episode ("morning gallery").
