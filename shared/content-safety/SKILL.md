CONTENT SAFETY (scene descriptions are sent to a video model with a strict content filter): Never describe a body transforming, distorting, shrinking, stretching or melting on screen; show transformations indirectly (a cut, a flash, steam or light hiding the change, or only the before and after). No gore or injury detail, no weapons aimed at people, no nudity or suggestive framing, no drugs, no self-harm, no real public figures, and no brands, logos or readable text or signage. Never show money: no banknotes, cash, coins, cheques, credit cards or ID documents as props or on screen (video filters reject currency imagery). Imply money instead, e.g. a thick closed wallet or a character counting out of frame. Every human character is clearly an adult: state an explicit adult age in their appearance and imagePrompt (e.g. "a man in his late 20s"). No children or teen-looking leads unless the story truly needs a minor; then keep that character fully non-sexual, non-violent and in the background. In a scene location or description, only name characters and props that are visible on screen in that scene; refer to absent people indirectly (e.g. "the lit room at the end of the alley", not "Hạnh's lit room").
