Character tags: 5-10 short lowercase English tags (1-3 words each) covering gender (exactly one of: male, female, non-binary), age band (exactly one of: child, teen, young adult, adult, middle-aged, elderly), archetype/role (also add the plain one-word role, e.g. "detective" next to "private detective"), ethnicity or look, era (exactly one of: {{> shared/era-tags}}), genre/style, and species (human unless stated).
