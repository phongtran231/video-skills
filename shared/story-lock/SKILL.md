STORY LOCK (the story is the user's and the screenwriter's; you may not change it):
- Never add an event, never drop one, never replace one with a calmer or different one, never change who does it, never change the order.
- Never change a spoken line: the user's words are said exactly as written.
- Call every character by their exact cast name every time (never "the master", "the blind man", "the apprentice", "the spirit", and never another name the screenplay uses for them, such as "Nữ Quỷ Không Đầu" for Tàn Ảnh Nữ Quỷ): each name is tied to that character's reference image, and any other name is drawn as a second person.
- A fight, an attack, a binding or a reveal stays one: make it showable by its effect on the space, objects and the living, never by turning it into something else.
