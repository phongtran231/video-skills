# Intro styles

One `### Name` per style (Vietnamese name, shown in the picker). The lines under it:

- `id`: stable slug (cover image: public/styles/intro/<id>.jpg).
- `hot`: yes = shown under "Đang hot".
- `description`: Vietnamese, for the picker.
- `direction`: English, the creative direction added to the storyboard prompt: the hook, how the video flows, camera and look. It shapes the video inside the format's rules (length, a presenter who speaks, the packshot at the end), never against them.
- `voice`: English, how the presenter talks.
- `cover`: English, the scene of the cover image (a generic product).
- `edit: calm`: no punch-in zooms or whooshes at the cuts (the format's `edit: punchy`), for slow styles.

### Review chân thật
id: honest-review
hot: yes
description: Một người cầm điện thoại tự quay, kể thật trải nghiệm dùng sản phẩm như đang nói chuyện với bạn thân.
direction: UGC selfie review filmed on a phone at arm's length in a real home. Hook: she holds the product up to the lens and gives her honest verdict in the first two seconds. Then quick handheld close-ups of her using it, natural daylight, slight phone shake, no studio polish. End with her holding it next to her face, smiling.
voice: casual, warm and candid, like talking to a close friend, a little fast
cover: a young woman filming a selfie video in her bright bedroom, holding a small product up to the phone camera and talking

### Đập hộp ASMR
id: unboxing-asmr
edit: calm
hot: yes
description: Mở hộp cận cảnh, tiếng giấy, tiếng nắp mở rõ từng chi tiết, rồi lần đầu dùng thử.
direction: Unboxing with crisp ASMR sounds. Hook: an extreme close-up of hands tearing the seal. Slow top-down and macro shots of the box opening, tissue paper, the product lifted out, every texture sharp. Then the presenter tries it for the first time on camera with a genuine reaction. Clean neutral desk, soft window light.
voice: soft, intimate, close to the microphone, with pauses for the sounds
cover: top-down close-up of hands opening a premium gift box on a clean wooden desk, tissue paper and a product inside

### Đừng mua nếu…
id: dont-buy-if
hot: yes
description: Mở đầu ngược đời "Đừng mua nếu…", gây tò mò rồi lật lại bằng lý do nên mua.
direction: Reverse hook: the presenter looks straight into the lens and says "don't buy this if…" with a raised eyebrow. Quick jump cuts as she lists two playful conditions, then flips it: the real reasons it is worth it, each with a product close-up. Fast pace, bold confident framing, eye-level medium shots.
voice: confident and teasing, punchy short sentences, a playful twist
cover: a confident young woman pointing at the camera with a raised eyebrow, holding a product in her other hand, bright modern room

### Vấn đề và giải pháp
id: problem-solution
hot: yes
description: Mở bằng nỗi khổ quen thuộc, rồi sản phẩm xuất hiện giải quyết gọn gàng.
direction: Problem then solution. Hook: a relatable everyday frustration acted out in a short, slightly exaggerated moment (cooler, desaturated look). Then the product enters and the light warms up as it solves the problem, shown clearly in use. End on the relieved, happy presenter with the product.
voice: empathetic at first, then upbeat and relieved
cover: split mood scene, a frustrated woman on the left in dull light and the same woman smiling with a product in warm light on the right

### Trước và sau
id: before-after
hot: yes
description: Cho thấy rõ khác biệt trước khi dùng và sau khi dùng, kết quả nói thay lời quảng cáo.
direction: Before and after. Hook: the "before" state shown honestly, then a clean transition (a swipe of the hand or a turn) into the "after" with the product in use. Same framing and light for both so the difference is clear. Keep claims visual and believable, no exaggerated results.
voice: matter-of-fact and pleased, lets the result speak
cover: a tidy before and after comparison, the same countertop messy on one side and clean and bright on the other with a product in the middle

### Livestream chốt đơn
id: livestream-sale
hot: yes
description: Như đang livestream bán hàng: nói nhanh, cầm sản phẩm sát camera, khoe ưu đãi.
direction: Livestream selling style. The presenter sits at a table facing the camera with a ring light, products arranged around her, energetic. Hook: she lifts the product right up to the lens and announces the deal. Quick demos at the table, holding it close to show details, counting the benefits on her fingers. Bright even light, slightly wide lens.
voice: high energy, fast, enthusiastic seller, friendly urgency
cover: a cheerful woman at a table with a ring light, holding a product close to the camera like a livestream seller, products around her

### Top 3 lý do
id: top-3-reasons
description: Đếm ngược 3 lý do nên dùng, mỗi lý do một cảnh rõ ràng.
direction: Countdown of three reasons. Hook: the presenter holds up three fingers. Each reason gets its own short scene with the product shown doing that thing, the presenter counting down on her fingers between them. Clean, bright, well organised framing.
voice: clear and upbeat, like a friendly explainer
cover: a smiling woman holding up three fingers next to a product on a clean bright table

### POV phát hiện
id: pov-discovery
description: Góc nhìn thứ nhất: "POV: bạn vừa tìm ra món này", máy quay là mắt người xem.
direction: POV, first-person camera. Hook: the viewer's own hands find the product, as if they just discovered it. Shots from the eye-line of the user: hands picking it up, using it, then turning the camera to a friend who reacts and explains why it is great. Natural, immersive, handheld.
voice: excited friend talking to the viewer, natural and spontaneous
cover: first person view of hands holding a product in a cosy apartment, a friend smiling in the background

### Phỏng vấn đường phố
id: street-interview
description: Cầm micro hỏi người qua đường, người được hỏi dùng thử và nói cảm nhận.
direction: Street interview. Hook: a presenter holding a small microphone stops someone on a lively city street and asks a quick question about the product. The passer-by tries it on the spot and reacts honestly. Handheld, eye-level, city bokeh, daylight.
voice: lively, spontaneous, real conversation
cover: a presenter holding a small microphone interviewing a smiling woman on a busy sunny city street, the woman holding a product

### Chuyên gia chia sẻ
id: expert-tips
description: Một người am hiểu giải thích vì sao sản phẩm tốt, rõ ràng và đáng tin.
direction: A knowledgeable presenter explains how the product works in a calm, credible setting (a clean workspace or studio). Hook: one surprising fact about the product, said to camera. Close-ups of details, materials or ingredients while she explains. No medical or exaggerated claims, only what the product information says.
voice: calm, clear, trustworthy, measured pace
cover: a professional-looking woman in a clean bright studio explaining a product she holds, a neat desk behind her

### Một ngày dùng thử
id: day-in-life
description: Sản phẩm đi theo người dùng suốt một ngày, sáng đến tối.
direction: A day in the life. Quick moments from morning to evening (home, commute, work, evening), the product naturally part of each. Hook: the alarm or the first coffee with the product in frame. Warm natural light changing through the day, smooth handheld.
voice: relaxed narrator sharing her day, friendly
cover: a young woman in morning light at home with coffee and a product on the table, cosy lifestyle feel

### Studio cao cấp
id: luxury-studio
edit: calm
description: Phong cách quảng cáo cao cấp: nền tối, ánh sáng điêu khắc, chuyển động chậm.
direction: Premium commercial look. Dark seamless studio, sculpted rim light, slow motorised camera moves, slow motion. Hook: the product revealed from darkness by a sweep of light. The presenter, elegant and composed, speaks a few refined lines to camera between macro product shots.
voice: elegant, slow, confident, a touch of luxury
cover: a luxury product on a dark reflective surface lit by a dramatic beam of light, elegant woman in soft focus behind

### Điện ảnh
id: cinematic
edit: calm
description: Khung hình như phim: ánh sáng đẹp, ống kính rộng, màu điện ảnh.
direction: Cinematic short film. Anamorphic-feel wide shots, golden hour or moody practical light, shallow depth of field, a filmic colour grade. Hook: a striking establishing shot with the presenter and the product. Slow dolly moves and a short story arc; the presenter speaks briefly to camera like a film character.
voice: warm, cinematic, unhurried
cover: a cinematic golden hour scene of a woman on a balcony holding a product, shallow depth of field, film look

### Tình huống hài
id: comedy-skit
description: Một tình huống hài ngắn, sản phẩm cứu nguy đúng lúc.
direction: A short comedy skit with two people. Hook: an awkward, funny situation in the first seconds. The product saves the day in a surprising way; reactions are big but believable. Bright, colourful, sitcom-style framing with quick cuts.
voice: playful, comic timing, expressive
cover: two friends laughing in a bright kitchen, one holding up a product triumphantly

### Rủ bạn cùng dùng
id: friends-together
description: Hai người bạn cùng dùng thử, trò chuyện tự nhiên, vui vẻ.
direction: Two friends try the product together at home or a café. Hook: one surprises the other with it. Natural banter, both try it, react and recommend it to the camera together. Warm, candid, handheld.
voice: two friendly voices, natural, fun
cover: two young women friends sitting on a sofa laughing and trying a product together

### Thử độ bền
id: durability-test
description: Thử thách sản phẩm: rơi, nước, nhiệt… cho thấy chất lượng thật.
direction: A durability or performance test. Hook: the presenter announces the test and does something bold with the product (a drop, water, heat, a long day), filmed clearly in slow motion. Then she shows it still works perfectly. Only tests that suit this product and are safe.
voice: curious and impressed, a little dramatic
cover: a product splashing into water in slow motion on a clean bright table, a woman watching in surprise

### Mẹo hay
id: life-hack
description: Một mẹo nhỏ hữu ích gắn với sản phẩm, xem xong muốn thử ngay.
direction: Life hack format. Hook: "here's a trick nobody tells you". Quick step-by-step close-ups showing a clever way to use the product, the presenter demonstrating with her hands and talking to camera. Clean, bright, top-down and eye-level mix.
voice: clever friend sharing a secret, quick and clear
cover: close-up of hands demonstrating a clever trick with a product on a bright kitchen counter

### Chỉ có đôi tay
id: hands-only
edit: calm
description: Không lộ mặt, chỉ đôi tay thao tác cận cảnh, tập trung vào sản phẩm.
direction: Hands-only demo. Every shot is a tight close-up of hands using the product on a clean surface, top-down and 45°, satisfying textures and motion. The presenter is heard but never seen (her lines are voice-over); she appears only in the final shot, holding the product.
voice: calm, smooth voice-over, satisfying

### Câu chuyện thương hiệu
id: brand-story
edit: calm
description: Kể vì sao sản phẩm ra đời, chân thành và có cảm xúc.
direction: Founder or brand story. The presenter tells, to camera, why this product exists, in a workshop or a warm home setting, intercut with craft details of the product being made or prepared. Hook: a personal first line. Warm, authentic, documentary feel.
voice: sincere, heartfelt, storytelling pace
cover: a woman in a warm workshop with natural light, holding a product and smiling, tools and materials around her

### Bày biện từ trên xuống
id: flatlay
edit: calm
description: Sản phẩm bày trên mặt bàn nhìn từ trên xuống, gọn gàng và rất "aesthetic".
direction: Flatlay style. Top-down styled arrangements of the product with matching props on a coloured or textured surface, hands entering the frame to rearrange and use it. Hook: a satisfying arrangement snapping into place. The presenter speaks to camera in a short medium shot between flatlays.
voice: soft, aesthetic, friendly
cover: aesthetic top-down flatlay of a product with matching props on a pastel surface

### Deal sốc
