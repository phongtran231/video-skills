# Try-on styles

One `### Name` per style (Vietnamese name, shown in the picker). Same fields as skills/formats/intro/styles.md:
`id`, `hot`, `description` (Vietnamese), `direction` (English, added to the storyboard prompt inside the format's
rules), `voice` (English), `edit: calm` (no punch-ins or whooshes at the cuts) and `cover` (English, the scene of the cover image: public/styles/tryon/<id>.jpg).

### Phòng thử đồ
id: fitting-room-haul
hot: yes
description: Mặc thử từng món trong phòng thử đồ, bước ra khoe và nhận xét thật lòng.
direction: Try-on haul in a fitting room. Hook: she pulls the curtain open and steps out in the first outfit. Mirror shots and medium shots as she turns, shows the fit, the fabric and the details, then her honest opinion to camera. Warm boutique light, handheld phone feel.
voice: chatty, honest, excited about the clothes
cover: a young woman stepping out of a boutique fitting room curtain in a stylish outfit, smiling at the camera

### Một món phối ba kiểu

hot: yes

voice: creative stylist, upbeat, inspiring
